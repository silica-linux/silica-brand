The brand of [Silica](https://github.com/silica-linux/silica-desktop): the logo (with the name set
in Inter), the repository banners and, later, the release wallpapers. Every file has its row in
`ASSETS.md` (author, licence, source).

Clean-room rules as in silica-icons: no shape that recalls the marks of other systems.

## The mark: from sand to glass

A circle split along its vertical diameter. On the left, grains of sand; on the right, solid
glass. Silica is both: the sand on the beach and the glass it melts into. The grains grow towards
the diameter, as if they were about to fuse.

```
exports/
  mark/          mark SVGs: colour light, colour dark, mono (currentColor), black, white;
                 pixel-drawn 16, 24 and 32 px variants
  png/           mark PNGs at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 (light and dark)
  logotype/      horizontal and vertical logotypes: light, dark, mono, black, white (text as outlines)
  banners/       GitHub banners, 1280 × 640, one per repository (SVG and PNG)
  app-icon/      the mark as an app icon in the silica-icons format (layers and previews)
  construction/  construction drawing and clear space
design/
  silica-brand.sketch   pages Construction, Mark, Wordmark, App Icon, Banners
tools/           generator for the exports (Python)
```

The exports are generated by `tools/build.py`; the geometry lives in `tools/geometry.py`. They are
the source of truth. The Sketch file presents them and uses them (symbols for the mark, the
logotypes and the banner).

## Construction

A 64-unit (u) grid on the mark's canvas; on the 1024 canvas, 1 u = 16 px.

| Element | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Circle | radius 28 u (diameter 56), centred on the canvas; margin 4 u per side |
| Glass | the right half disc, from the vertical diameter |
| Grain grid | hexagonal, pitch 5.2 u: columns 4.503 u apart (5.2 · √3/2), odd columns shifted by half a pitch, so every grain has six neighbours at the same distance |
| Columns | six; the first is 3.6 u left of the diameter |
| Grain size | radius 2.2 u in the first column, falling linearly to 0.85 u in the sixth (43 grains) |
| Clipping | a grain is kept only if it lies wholly inside the circle, 0.4 u in from the outline |
| Transition | clear gap of 1.4 u between the first column and the glass, 1.75 × the gap between the grains of that column (0.8 u): the glass reads as one body, the sand as separate grains |

### Small variants

At 16, 24 and 32 px the grains of the reference drawing are too small to read, so these sizes
have their own drawings on the pixel grid: a few large grains, squares of whole pixels with
rounded corners, largest next to the diameter, with at least one clear pixel between grains and
between the first column and the glass. The diameter falls on a pixel edge.

| Variant | Circle | Grains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 px | radius 7, centre (8, 8) | 3: two of 3 px, one of 2 px |
| 24 px | radius 10.5, centre (12, 12) | 5: three of 4 px, two of 3 px |
| 32 px | radius 14, centre (16, 16) | 10: three of 4 px, four of 3 px, three of 2 px |

Use each variant only at its own size; from 48 px up use the reference drawing.

## Logotype

The name "Silica" in Inter SemiBold (600), tracking −0.02 em. With D = circle diameter:

- **Horizontal**: cap height 0.5 D; the centre of the x-height sits on the centre of the circle
  (the optical centre of a circle is its geometric centre); the name starts 0.25 D to the right
  of the circle.
- **Vertical**: cap height 0.3 D; the cap height starts 0.25 D below the circle; the name's ink
  is centred on the circle's vertical axis (the diameter).

The rule is the same for light, dark and monochrome. Measured offsets from the rule, in the
exports rendered at 10× and in the Sketch symbols: 0.005 px or less.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use | Lowest contrast (WCAG 2.2) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sand | `#A87432` | grains on light | 4.03 on white, 3.73 on Paper, 3.50 on `#ECEFF4` |
| Glass | `#2F6FE4` | glass on light | 4.65 on white, 4.30 on Paper, 4.04 on `#ECEFF4` |
| Sand on dark | `#E2BE84` | grains on dark | 10.10 on Ink, 8.08 on Night |
| Glass on dark | `#5B9BFF` | glass on dark | 6.41 on Ink, 5.13 on Night |
| Ink | `#16181C` | text on light, dark background | |
| Paper | `#F5F6F8` | text on dark, light background | |
| Night | `#1B2A4A` → `#0B1426` | dark app icon background | |

Every colour of the mark has at least 3:1 contrast with the background it sits on (WCAG 2.2,
non-text contrast), on white and Paper, on Ink, and on both app icon backgrounds.

## Usage

- **Mark alone**: system icon, favicon, repository avatars.
- **Horizontal logotype**: headers, website, banners.
- **Vertical logotype**: covers and square spaces.
- **Colour on light** (`*-light`) on white or light backgrounds, **colour on dark** (`*-dark`) on
  Ink or darker backgrounds.
- **Monochrome** when colour is unavailable or does not read: black on light, white on dark or
  on photos; `*-mono.svg` uses `currentColor`.

## Clear space

Leave a margin of **D/4** on every side of the mark and the logotypes (D = circle diameter). No
text, rules or other marks inside it.

## Minimum size

- Mark: **16 px** (with the 16 px variant); 5 mm across in print.
- Logotype: circle at least **24 px** across (horizontal logotype 67 px wide); 20 mm wide in
  print.

## Don'ts

- Don't stretch, rotate, skew or mirror the mark: sand is always on the left, glass on the right.
- Don't change the colours, and don't use the light colours on dark backgrounds or the other way
  round: the contrast falls below 3:1.
- Don't rearrange, add or remove grains, and don't fill the gap between sand and glass.
- Don't scale the reference drawing below 48 px: use the 16, 24 and 32 px variants, each only at
  its own size.
- Don't add shadows, outlines, reflections or glass effects to the mark.
- Don't reset the name in another typeface, weight or tracking; don't write "SILICA" in capitals
  in the logotype.
- Don't change the proportions between mark and name or the gap between them.
- Don't put the colour mark on mid-contrast backgrounds or on photos: use the monochrome mark.
- Don't place it next to the marks of other systems or use it for products that are not Silica.

## App icon

`exports/app-icon/` follows the silica-icons format (1024 canvas, body 824, keyline `circle`).
The circle is 640 across, 1.067 × the 600 keyline: a circle as wide as a square covers only π/4 of
its area and looks smaller, so it overshoots the keyline. The Default theme uses the light
background (`#FFFFFF` → `#ECEFF4`) with the light colours; the Dark theme uses Night (`#1B2A4A` →
`#0B1426`) with the dark colours.

## Rebuilding the exports

You need the Inter variable font (OFL, [rsms/inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), v4) outside
the repository:

```
python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
venv/bin/python tools/build.py --inter <path>/Inter-VariableFont_opsz,wght.ttf
```
