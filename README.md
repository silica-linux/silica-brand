# silica-brand

The brand of [Silica](https://github.com/silica-linux/silica-desktop): the logo (with the name set
in Inter), the repository banners and, later, the release wallpapers. Every file has its row in
`ASSETS.md` (author, licence, source).

Clean-room rules as in silica-icons: no shape that recalls the marks of other systems.

## The mark

An SiO₄ tetrahedron seen from above: the basic unit of silica, a silicon atom at the centre bonded
to four oxygens. The triangle is the outline, the centre is the silicon, the three spokes are the
bonds; the three faces take their light from the top left.

```
exports/
  mark/          mark SVGs: colour light, colour dark, mono (currentColor), black, white;
                 pixel-hinted 16, 24 and 32 px variants
  png/           mark PNGs at 16, 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 (light and dark)
  logotype/      horizontal and vertical logotypes: light, dark, mono, black, white (text as outlines)
  banners/       GitHub banners, 1280 × 640, one per repository (SVG and PNG)
  app-icon/      the mark as an app icon in the silica-icons format (layers and previews)
  construction/  construction drawing and clear space
design/
  silica-brand.sketch   pages Construction, Mark, Wordmark, App Icon, Banners
tools/           generator for the exports (Python)
```

The exports are generated by `tools/build.py`, which holds the geometry: they are the source of
truth. The Sketch file presents them and uses them (symbols for the mark, the logotypes and the
banner).

## Construction

A 64-unit (u) grid on the mark's canvas; on the 1024 canvas, 1 u = 16 px.

| Element | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Median triangle (axis of the edges) | equilateral, side 53.20 u, apex (32, 4.75), base at y 50.82 |
| Centre | centroid of the median triangle, (32, 35.46); the three spokes join it to the vertices |
| Silhouette | the median triangle grown by 2.5 u, corner radius 4 u; bounds 56 × 49.57 u, side margin 4 u |
| Outline | 5 u |
| Spokes to the centre | 4.5 u (0.9 of the outline: three strokes meeting at 120° make a heavier knot than an edge) |
| Faces | corner radius 1.25 u at the acute corners and 1.5 u at the centre; together they span x 14.15–49.85, y 17.06–48.32 |
| Optical centring | the point halfway between the centre of the silhouette's bounding box (y 28.54) and the centroid (y 35.46) sits on the canvas centre: the box rises by 3.46 u (about h/12) |

The mark is made of fills only: the silhouette minus the three faces gives the outline and the
three spokes (the monochrome mark); the colour mark fills the faces and paints the rest in the
rim colour.

### Small variants

Below 48 px use the variants drawn on the pixel grid, each at its own exact size:

| Variant | Outline (base / sides) | Vertical spoke | Diagonal spokes | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 16 px | 1 / 1.1 px | 1 px, column 7 | 1 px | base on row 13–14, axis at x 7.5 (0.5 px left of the canvas centre, to keep the vertical spoke sharp) |
| 24 px | 2 / 2.1 px | 2 px, columns 11–12 | 1.8 px | base on rows 18–20 |
| 32 px | 2 / 2.5 px | 2 px, columns 15–16 | 2.2 px | base on rows 25–27 |

Horizontal and vertical lines fall on whole pixels; the diagonal sides are slightly heavier than
the base because antialiasing lightens them. In these variants the triangle is equilateral to
within half a pixel.

## Logotype

The name "Silica" in Inter SemiBold (600), tracking −0.02 em. It aligns to the **faces** of the
mark, the part that shows on every background (in both colour versions the rim blends into the
background). The rule is the same for light, dark and monochrome. With H = silhouette height:

- **Horizontal**: cap height 0.5 H; baseline on the bottom edge of the lower face; the name starts
  0.3 H to the right of the faces.
- **Vertical**: cap height 0.382 H (H / φ²); the cap height starts 0.3 H below the lower face; the
  name's ink is centred on the mark's axis.

## Usage

- **Mark alone**: system icon, favicon, repository avatars.
- **Horizontal logotype**: headers, website, banners.
- **Vertical logotype**: covers and square spaces.
- **Colour on light** (`*-light`) on light backgrounds, **colour on dark** (`*-dark`) on dark
  backgrounds; the rim blends with the background and the faces stay separate.
- **Monochrome** when colour is unavailable or does not read: black on light, white on dark or
  on photos; `*-mono.svg` uses `currentColor`.

| Silica Light | `#A9CCFF` | left face |
| Silica Blue | `#5B9BFF` | right face |
| Silica Deep | `#2F6FE4` | lower face |
| Ink | `#16181C` | text on light, dark background; rim on dark at 55 % |
| Paper | `#F5F6F8` | text on dark, light background |
| Rim on light | `#FFFFFF` at 92 % | outline and spokes of the light version |

## Clear space

Leave a margin of **H/4** on every side of the mark and the logotypes (H = silhouette height).
No text, rules or other marks inside it.

## Minimum size

- Mark: **16 px** (with the 16 variant); 5 mm tall in print.
- Logotype: mark at least **24 px** tall (horizontal logotype 66 px wide); 20 mm wide in print.

## Don'ts

- Don't stretch, rotate, skew or mirror the mark.
- Don't change the colours of the faces or their order (the light comes from the top left).
- Don't add shadows, outlines, reflections or glass effects to the mark: glass belongs to the
  interface, not the logo.
- Don't reset the name in another typeface, weight or tracking; don't write "SILICA" in capitals
  in the logotype.
- Don't change the proportions between mark and name or the gap between them.
- Don't use the 16, 24 and 32 variants at sizes other than their own, and don't go below the
  minimum size.
- Don't put the colour mark on mid-contrast backgrounds or on photos: use the monochrome mark.
- Don't place it next to the marks of other systems or use it for products that are not Silica.

## App icon

`exports/app-icon/` follows the silica-icons format (1024 canvas, body 824). The Default theme
pairs the light background (`#FFFFFF` → `#ECEFF4`) with the dark-rim mark; the Dark theme pairs
graphite (`#3A3A3C` → `#161618`) with the light-rim mark.

## Rebuilding the exports

You need the Inter variable font (OFL, [rsms/inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), v4) outside
the repository:

```
python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
venv/bin/python tools/build.py --inter <path>/Inter-VariableFont_opsz,wght.ttf
```
