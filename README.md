The brand of [Silica](https://github.com/silica-linux/silica-desktop): the logo (with the name set
in Inter), the repository banners and the desktop wallpapers. Every file has its row in
`ASSETS.md` (author, licence, source).

Clean-room rules as in silica-icons: no shape that recalls the marks of other systems.

## The logo: from sand to glass

The Silica logo is an **app icon** of the silica-icons family, drawn like Mail and Mustache: a
body of 824 on a 1024 canvas with its own gradient background, and in the foreground a circle
split along its vertical diameter, grains of sand on the left and solid glass on the right.
Silica is both: the sand on the beach and the glass it melts into. The grains grow towards the
diameter, as if they were about to fuse.

The circle alone, flat and without the body, is the **flat mark**: for 16 px, favicons,
monochrome and one-colour printing.

```
exports/
  app-icon/      the logo as an app icon in the silica-icons format: background, foreground
                 and their -dark variants, icon.json, previews
  png/           the icon at 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, 1024 (light and dark, 1024 canvas);
                 the flat mark at 16, 32, 64 (light and dark)
  logotype/      icon + name, horizontal and vertical, light and dark; flat mark + name in
                 one colour (mono, black, white); text as outlines
  mark/          flat mark SVGs: colour light, colour dark, mono (currentColor), black, white;
                 pixel-drawn 16, 24 and 32 px variants
  banners/       GitHub banners, 1280 × 640, one per repository (SVG and PNG)
  wallpapers/    the default desktop wallpaper, light and dark, 2560 × 1440 and @2x (PNG),
                 with its recipe in wallpapers.json (gradient, glows, blur)
  construction/  construction drawing and clear space of the flat mark
design/
  silica-brand.sketch   pages Construction, Mark, Wordmark, App Icon, Banners
tools/           generator for the exports (Python)
```

The exports are generated by `tools/build.py`; the geometry lives in `tools/geometry.py`, the
icon in `tools/icon.py`. They are the source of truth. The Sketch file presents them and uses
them (symbols for the icon, the flat mark, the logotypes and the banner).

## Construction of the flat mark

A 64-unit (u) grid on the mark's canvas; on the 1024 canvas, 1 u = 16 px.

| Element | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Circle | radius 28 u (diameter 56), centred on the canvas; margin 4 u per side |
| Glass | the right half disc, from the vertical diameter |
| Grain grid | hexagonal, pitch 5.2 u: columns 4.503 u apart (5.2 · √3/2), odd columns shifted by half a pitch, so every grain has six neighbours at the same distance |
| Columns | six; the first is 3.6 u left of the diameter |
| Grain size | radius 2.2 u in the first column, falling linearly to 0.85 u in the sixth (43 grains) |
| Clipping | a grain is kept only if it lies wholly inside the circle, 0.4 u in from the outline |
| Transition | clear gap of 1.4 u between the first column and the glass, 1.75 × the gap between the grains of that column (0.8 u): the glass reads as one body, the sand as separate grains |

### Small variants

At 16, 24 and 32 px the grains of the reference drawing are too small to read, so these sizes
have their own drawings on the pixel grid: a few large grains, squares of whole pixels with
rounded corners, largest next to the diameter, with at least one clear pixel between grains and
between the first column and the glass. The diameter falls on a pixel edge.

| Variant | Circle | Grains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 px | radius 7, centre (8, 8) | 3: two of 3 px, one of 2 px |
| 24 px | radius 10.5, centre (12, 12) | 5: three of 4 px, two of 3 px |
| 32 px | radius 14, centre (16, 16) | 10: three of 4 px, four of 3 px, three of 2 px |

Use each variant only at its own size; from 48 px up use the reference drawing.

## The app icon

Layers in `exports/app-icon/`, in the silica-icons format (keyline `circle`, foreground shadow
`neutral`). The background fills the 824 body (the body shape of the silica-icons spec); the
foreground carries the circle, 640 across: 1.067 × the 600 keyline, because a circle as wide as a
square covers only π/4 of its area and looks smaller.

| Part | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#FFFFFF` → `#DDE3EC` (as Mail) | `#3A3A3C` → `#141416` (as Mail) |
| Glass | `#2F74E8` → `#1652D0`, white sheen 28 % → 0 | `#6AAAFF` → `#4A90FF`, sheen 22 % |
| Sand (each grain) | `#A06A26` → `#83541A` | `#EFCB90` → `#C9A060` |
| Shadow under glass / grains | `#0B2D6A` 22 %, y 8, blur 10 / warm 30 %, y 3, blur 2.5 | black 40 % |
| Inner light edge | white 55 %, y 5 (glass), y 2 (grains) | white 40 % |

Mask, rim and outer shadow come from the shell, as for every app icon. At 32 px the icon uses the
pixel-drawn 24 px mark, scaled so that its grains fall on whole pixels.

## Logotype

The icon (or, in one colour, the flat mark) and the name "Silica" in Inter SemiBold (600),
tracking −0.02 em. With B = side of the icon body (for the flat mark, D = circle diameter):

- **Horizontal**: cap height 0.42 B; the centre of the x-height sits on the centre of the body;
  the name starts 0.24 B to the right of the body.
- **Vertical**: cap height 0.25 B; the cap height starts 0.2 B below the body; the name's ink is
  centred on the body's vertical axis.

Measured offsets from the rule: 0.000 px in the Sketch symbols; in the exports rendered at 10×,
0.015 px for the x-height centre and 0.04 px for the gap (horizontal), 0.00 px for the axis
(vertical).

## Colours

Every colour has at least 3:1 contrast with the background it sits on (WCAG 2.2, non-text
contrast). For the icon the check uses the worst pair of gradient stops.

| Use | Colour | Lowest contrast |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Icon, light: glass | `#2F74E8` → `#1652D0` | 3.40 on `#DDE3EC` |
| Icon, light: sand | `#A06A26` → `#83541A` | 3.55 on `#DDE3EC` |
| Icon, dark: glass | `#6AAAFF` → `#4A90FF` | 3.63 on `#3A3A3C` |
| Icon, dark: sand | `#EFCB90` → `#C9A060` | 4.69 on `#3A3A3C` |
| Flat mark on light: Sand / Glass | `#A87432` / `#2F6FE4` | 3.73 / 4.30 on Paper `#F5F6F8` (4.03 / 4.65 on white) |
| Flat mark on dark: Sand / Glass | `#E2BE84` / `#5B9BFF` | 10.10 / 6.41 on Ink `#16181C` |
| Text | Ink `#16181C` on light, Paper `#F5F6F8` on dark | |

## Usage

- **App icon (the logo)**: about boxes, websites, repository avatars, the logotype, banners; from
  32 px up.
- **Horizontal logotype**: headers, website, banners. **Vertical logotype**: covers, square spaces.
- **Flat mark**: 16 px, favicons, monochrome, one-colour printing, embossing. Colour on light
  (`*-light`) on white or light backgrounds, colour on dark (`*-dark`) on Ink or darker; black or
  white in one colour; `*-mono.svg` uses `currentColor`.

## Clear space

**B/4** around the icon and the logotypes (B = side of the icon body); **D/4** around the flat
mark (D = circle diameter). No text, rules or other marks inside it.

## Minimum size

- App icon: **32 px** (with the pixel-drawn mark inside).
- Flat mark: **16 px** (with the 16 px variant); 5 mm across in print.
- Logotype: icon body at least **24 px** (horizontal logotype 61 px wide); 20 mm wide in print.

## Don'ts

- Don't stretch, rotate, skew or mirror the logo: sand is always on the left, glass on the right.
- Don't take the circle out of the body to use it in colour at large sizes: the logo is the icon;
  the flat mark is for small sizes and one colour.
- Don't change the colours, and don't use the light colours on dark backgrounds or the other way
  round: the contrast falls below 3:1.
- Don't rearrange, add or remove grains, and don't fill the gap between sand and glass.
- Don't scale the flat reference drawing below 48 px: use the 16, 24 and 32 px variants, each only
  at its own size.
- Don't add effects to the flat mark; don't add rim or outer shadow to the icon layers (the shell
  adds them).
- Don't reset the name in another typeface, weight or tracking; don't write "SILICA" in capitals.
- Don't change the proportions between icon and name or the gap between them.
- Don't place it next to the marks of other systems or use it for products that are not Silica.

## Rebuilding the exports

You need the Inter variable font (OFL, [rsms/inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), v4) outside
the repository:

```
python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install -r tools/requirements.txt
venv/bin/python tools/build.py --inter <path>/Inter-VariableFont_opsz,wght.ttf
```
