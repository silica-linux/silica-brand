# Silica trademark policy

The Silica name and logo identify the Silica project. The code of every Silica repository is
open source (MIT OR Apache-2.0, icons CC BY-SA 4.0); the **name and the logo are not**: they are
trademarks of the project, and the files in this repository are © the Silica project, all
rights reserved, except as allowed below.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name and the unmodified logo to refer to Silica: articles, reviews, talks, videos,
  tutorials, links to the project, screenshots of Silica itself.
- Show the logo in software that integrates with Silica to point to it (e.g. a "Silica" session
  entry in a login screen), as long as it is clear that it is not your product.
- Use the files of this repository unmodified in those contexts.

## You may not

- Modify the logo, combine it with other marks, or use parts of it in your own logo.
- Use the name or the logo for a modified version, a fork or a derived distribution: those keep
  the code but must remove the Silica branding (name, logo, wallpapers, the boot and login
  artwork) and use their own.
- Use the name or the logo in a way that suggests the Silica project endorses you, your product
  or your service.
- Register domains, trademarks or accounts that contain the name in a confusing way.

## Unmodified builds

Official builds and unmodified rebuilds of the official sources may keep the branding. If you
patch the software beyond packaging fixes, remove the branding.

## Asking

For anything not covered here, open an issue in this repository.
